Openpage mascots

50 fractal buddies (F01–F50) and 50 ducks (D01–D50). Every design is drawn from simple shapes and math by the code in this page, so they're all original. The ducks were made deliberately different from CS50's rubber duck: a lime feather tuft, note-taking props and their own proportions. Pick numbers you like, or mix features ("F23's body with F07's face").

Fractal buddies

10 fractal bodies × 5 faces.

Ducks

5 duck styles × 10 props.
